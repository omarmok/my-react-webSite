Vehicle Card

A one-vehicle summary with 13 states and two forms: Web and App.

Component: Vehicle CardFiles: _vehicle-card.scssLast updated: November 5, 2026

Overview

Figma: moi-vehicle-cardCode: .moi-vehicle-cardPanels and switching: included

The vehicle card summarizes one vehicle. In Figma it is the moi-vehicle-card component with 13 states (view) and two forms (Web and App); in code it is the .moi-vehicle-card component with the same names as the Figma layers.

Component objective

  • Show a vehicle at a glance in inquiry results and case records: plate, make, model, year and status.
  • Reach from the same card the owner data, owners sequence, users, circulars, vehicle record and traffic security.
  • One visual language with the Person Card.

Usage

  • Use it for a single vehicle. For a list of vehicles use the Table.
  • Do not create a separate vehicle layout: use .moi-vehicle-card and change only the fields and sections.

Web and App

The same component has two forms through the App property: Web (1003 wide in Full) and App (430 wide). In the App form buttons and fields become two columns and «Owner name» takes a full row at 100%; sections and interactions stay the same.

  • The App form is complete in 12 of the 13 views (Full and every Full-*); plate-wallet is still initial in App.
  • Section navigation uses prototype interactions: section buttons, «Show details» and «Close».

Section behaviour

  • Buttons sit in one row with no per-button colours; only the active one uses Primary, and labels are Text sm / Medium.
  • Sensitive owner and user data is partly masked with stars (***).
  • Tables use the smaller table font and a size-sm Tag for status; owners and users are shown as a timeline.
  • History follows the chosen sub-button, and two sections are never open at once.

Each card is live and works with the bundled script; the button under each example copies the full code (markup and script) to the clipboard.

Design guidelines

  • Order of identity fields: plate, make, model, year. Everything else goes in the details body.
  • One status tag in the header; use the tag colors of the system (Tags).
  • A warning (wanted, stolen) always appears in the notification line, in text, not only in color.

Accessibility

  • Plate numbers mix Arabic and Latin letters: wrap them in <bdi> so the order is not scrambled in RTL.
  • Icon-only buttons need aria-label («طباعة»).
  • The vehicle photo needs a meaningful alt.

RTL notes

  • The component is built for dir="rtl"; the button row starts from the right and the fields flow right to left.
  • Plate and chassis values stay left-to-right inside <bdi>.

Acceptance criteria

  • Uses only .moi-vehicle-card classes, Tags and Buttons; no hard-coded colour.
  • Plate and chassis wrapped in <bdi>; status is text; buttons labelled.

Do & Don't

Do

  • Use .moi-vehicle-card with its class names as in Figma.
  • Keep plate number and owner in the same positions on every screen.

Don't

  • Do not copy raw colours or spacing; use the system variables.
  • Do not show plate numbers in a different direction than the rest of the data.
