Modal

Focused composable dialog: header, scrollable body (max 800px) and footer actions.

المكوّن: النافذة الملفات: _modal.scss آخر تحديث: 25 أكتوبر 2026

Purpose & when to use

The modal is a focused dialog over the page: a short form, a confirmation with fields, an assignment, a preview. Code: Bootstrap .modal styled by css/common/_modal.scss. Figma: Modal Type=Composable with modal-title, modal-body (Body Content slot) and modal-footer (Actions slot); a screen with a modal uses page-structure Type=with-modal.

  • One message or one decision without fields → Sweet Alert. Long or multi-step content → a page.
  • No modal on top of another modal.

Usage rules

Do

  • Title = the action («إسناد الطلب إلى شعبة»); primary button = the verb («إسناد»).
  • Mark required fields only when the business source says so.
  • Keep content within the 800px body; otherwise move it to a page.

Don’t

  • Don’t open a modal from a modal.
  • Don’t add a second overlay — Bootstrap and page-structure with-modal provide it.
  • Don’t use the modal for a simple yes / no — use Sweet Alert.

Accessibility & RTL

  • Use role="dialog", aria-modal="true" and aria-labelledby pointing at the title (Bootstrap markup).
  • Focus moves into the modal on open and returns to the trigger on close; Esc closes.
  • The close button needs aria-label="إغلاق".
