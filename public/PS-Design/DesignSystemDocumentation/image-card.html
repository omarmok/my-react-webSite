Image Card

An attached picture with a pager, a title and Delete / View actions.

Component: imageCard-V2Source: Figma «image card»Last updated: November 2, 2026

Overview

Figma: imageCard-V2Code: .moi-image-card

The image card shows one attached picture with a pager, a short title and two actions: Delete and View. It is used for evidence photos and attachments, usually as a row of cards (Figma «Image Gallery»). The new .moi-image-card in css/components/_image-card.scss matches Figma. The older .imagecard in _common.scss stays for existing screens only and is not used for new work. Rebuild main.css after pulling.

Accessibility

  • Give the picture a meaningful alt; the title alone is not enough for people who cannot see it.
  • Make the pager reachable: previous/next buttons or swipe plus keyboard arrows, and announce the position («2 of 4»).
  • Both buttons carry visible text («حذف», «مشاهدة»); deleting must ask for confirmation (Sweet Alert).

RTL notes

  • Title and buttons align to the start side; the Delete button comes first, then View.
  • The pager dots stay in a left-to-right row centered at the bottom, in both directions.

Do & Don't

Do

  • Use one aspect ratio for all cards in a gallery.
  • Keep the title to one short line.

Don't

  • Do not add a third action; move extras to a menu.
  • Do not stretch images: the slider crops to cover.
