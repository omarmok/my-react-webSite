Page Title

Green title bar with page icon, title and page actions, under the breadcrumb.

المكوّن: عنوان الصفحة الملفات: _common.scss (.page__title) آخر تحديث: 25 أكتوبر 2026

Purpose & when to use

The green title bar at the top of every service page: page icon + title on the start side, page actions (back, print, add, extra) on the end side. Code: .page__title in css/common/_common.scss. Figma: pagetitle (page ❖ Components / Page Title) with Show back-btn, Show print-btn, Show add-btn, Show extra-btn; it sits inside page-structure under the breadcrumb.

Usage rules

Do

  • Use the same title as the breadcrumb’s current page and the sidebar item.
  • Put the main page actions here (in details pages: the request decision buttons).
  • Keep «عودة» as the last action on the end side.

Don’t

  • Don’t put edit / delete in the title bar of a details page.
  • Don’t use more than three actions; move the rest into the content.
